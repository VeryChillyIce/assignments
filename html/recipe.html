Cheesy Tomato Pasta

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella or parmesan
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh basil or parsley

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. Warm olive oil in a pan and cook the garlic for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook until soft and juicy.
  4. Stir in the cooked pasta and cheese.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs before serving.
